This commercial lease agreement between ABC Realty Pvt Ltd and TechNova 
Solutions LLP contains both standard commercial clauses and several areas of 
legal risk that the lessee should carefully evaluate. The two year lock in 
period on a five year lease is a significant financial commitment that prevents 
the lessee from exiting without penalty during the initial period and should be 
negotiated for reasonableness. The annual rent escalation of 7 percent 
compounded over five years represents a substantial increase in occupancy costs 
and the agreement does not provide for downward revision in case of market 
decline. The security deposit of Rs 10 lakh is substantial and the agreement 
does not specify the timeline or conditions for its refund upon lease 
termination which creates financial risk for the lessee. The indemnity clause 
requiring the lessee to indemnify the lessor against all legal claims and 
damages caused by employees is broadly worded and may expose the lessee to 
disproportionate liability. The usage restriction limiting the premises strictly 
to IT and ITES purposes without defining these terms may create disputes over 
permitted activities. The force majeure clause is standard and acceptable. The 
arbitration clause referencing the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 is a 
positive inclusion that provides a clear and legally sound dispute resolution 
mechanism. Overall this agreement carries medium to high risk primarily due to 
the lock in period indemnity scope and deposit terms.
