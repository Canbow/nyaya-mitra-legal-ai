This rental agreement between Rajesh Sharma and Priya Verma contains several 
high risk clauses that require careful review before signing. The security 
deposit of Rs 50000 is interest free and refundable but no specific timeline 
for refund is mentioned which violates standard tenancy norms and may lead to 
disputes. The subletting restriction clause may be void under Section 10 of 
the Transfer of Property Act 1882 as it absolutely restrains the tenant from 
transferring interest in the property. The one month termination notice period 
may not comply with Section 106 of the Transfer of Property Act which requires 
fifteen days notice for monthly tenancies. The clause allowing immediate 
termination for two months rent default without court intervention may infringe 
on tenant rights under the applicable state rent control legislation. The 
restriction on pets and structural changes without specifying compensation 
mechanisms places disproportionate obligations on the tenant. Overall this 
agreement carries high risk for the tenant and several clauses should be 
renegotiated or clarified before execution.
