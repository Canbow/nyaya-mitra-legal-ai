This co living agreement between UrbanNest Living Pvt Ltd and Rahul Mehta 
contains several clauses that carry medium to high legal risk for the occupant. 
The classification of the arrangement as a license to occupy rather than a 
lease is a significant legal distinction that strips the occupant of standard 
tenant protections available under state rent control laws and the Transfer of 
Property Act 1882. The operator's right to reassign rooms with prior notice 
does not specify what constitutes adequate prior notice leaving the occupant 
vulnerable to arbitrary room changes. The early exit fee of 50 percent of one 
month rent may be considered an unreasonable penalty clause under Section 74 
of the Indian Contract Act 1872 which limits penalty clauses to reasonable 
compensation. The digital acceptance clause where the agreement is executed 
electronically via app without physical signature raises enforceability concerns 
under the Indian Contract Act and the Information Technology Act 2000. The 
15 day notice period for exit is shorter than the protection offered under 
standard tenancy frameworks. The security deposit of Rs 10000 has no specified 
refund timeline or process which may lead to disputes. Occupants should seek 
legal advice on the license versus lease distinction before signing this 
agreement.
