This short term rental agreement between host Aman Gupta and guest John Smith 
for Beachside Villa Goa contains multiple high risk clauses that significantly 
disadvantage the guest. The liability disclaimer clause where the host disclaims 
all responsibility for loss of personal belongings and injuries during the stay 
is potentially void under the Consumer Protection Act 2019 as it attempts to 
exclude liability for negligence. The security deposit of Rs 20000 described as 
deductible for damages does not specify the process for damage assessment or 
the timeline for refund which may lead to arbitrary deductions. The cancellation 
policy providing only 50 percent refund within three days does not align with 
fair consumer practice standards under Indian consumer protection law. The 
immediate eviction clause for noise complaints without defining what constitutes 
a noise complaint gives the host unilateral and unchecked power to terminate 
occupancy without notice. The agreement lacks a formal dispute resolution 
mechanism which is a significant legal gap. Guests should negotiate clearer 
terms around liability deposit refunds and eviction procedures before accepting 
this agreement.
